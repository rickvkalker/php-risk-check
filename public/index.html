Is your website's PHP safe and up to date?

We check whether your site runs an out-of-date or vulnerable version of PHP — including the critical php2shell flaw CERT-EU warns about — and tell you what to do. Version data comes live from php.net.

Passive check: we load a few standard pages on your site and read what they reveal. Nothing is attacked. For a definite answer, run the self-check on your own server.

This tool gives an indication, not a guarantee. It can't see PHP versions that are hidden, and hosts sometimes backport fixes without changing the version number. When in doubt, ask your hosting company or check php.net.
